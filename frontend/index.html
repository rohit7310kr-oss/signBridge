The Communication Gap

Breaking Barriers with Respect & Technology

Every day, millions of Deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals encounter friction when interacting in spaces designed almost exclusively around spoken language.

Communication Gaps

Everyday conversations can become difficult when people do not share a common sign language or interpreter, creating unintentional distance between individuals.

Focus: Mutual understanding & shared tools

Limited Accessibility

Important interactions may be harder in public spaces, medical desks, transport hubs, education, and everyday service counters without instant sign support.

Focus: Public services & everyday touchpoints

Technology for Inclusion

Modern computer vision offers an opportunity to explore lightweight, accessible tools that run directly in web browsers to assist sign interpretation.

Focus: AI-assisted assistive technologies

Architecture & Process

How signBridge Interprets Gestures

A privacy-first pipeline that turns live video frames into structured coordinate landmarks and text predictions inside your browser.

1

1. Sign

The user performs a hand gesture or fingerspelled letter directly in front of their standard camera or webcam.

Zero-delay client video stream
2

2. Recognize

Computer vision analyzes 21 key hand joint coordinates in real-time using lightweight browser-based inferencing.

21 Hand Landmarks Extracted
3

3. Understand

The system maps hand poses to supported character or word outputs and presents clear text results on screen.

Output: Clear Text Translation

Capabilities Overview

Built for Speed, Privacy, & Visual Clarity

Camera-Based Interaction

Project Status & Scope Transparency Prototype Notice

signBridge is an experimental prototype built for hackathon exploration. Current recognition capabilities focus on ASL fingerspelling (letter-by-letter spelling) and select hand landmarks. It does not provide full natural sign language sentence translation or universal sign vocabulary. Video stays strictly inside your browser.
